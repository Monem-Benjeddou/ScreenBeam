# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- be respectful and welcoming
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- focus on what's best for the community
- harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language aren't tolerated

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@Monem-Benjeddou](https://github.com/Monem-Benjeddou), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that don't follow this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.
